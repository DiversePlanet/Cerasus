1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cerasus website handles information when you visit or use the site.

Cerasus is a Minecraft city project and informational website associated with the Rulercraft server. The website is primarily intended to present information about the city, its history, projects, landmarks and ways to visit Cerasus in-game.

2. Information We Collect

Based on the current functionality of this website, Cerasus does not intentionally collect personal information through user accounts, registration forms, surveys, payment forms or other data-submission systems.

The website currently does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to browse its content.

3. Email Contact

The website provides an email address that can be used to contact the Cerasus city administration.

If you choose to contact us by email, the information you voluntarily include in your message may be received and used for the purpose of responding to your request.

We do not use submitted emails for unrelated purposes or intentionally sell or rent them to third parties.

4. Automatically Collected Information

The current Cerasus website code does not contain a dedicated analytics system, advertising network, user tracking system or custom visitor database.

However, standard technical information may potentially be processed by the web hosting provider, browser, network, content-delivery services or other infrastructure used to deliver the website. Such information can include technical information such as an IP address, browser type, device information or request information.

Cerasus does not currently use this information to create individual user profiles.

5. Cookies

The current Cerasus website does not intentionally place advertising, tracking or analytics cookies through the HTML and JavaScript provided for the site.

Third-party services used to host or deliver the website may nevertheless use their own technical mechanisms. Those services are responsible for their own policies and practices.

6. Third-Party Services

The website currently references external resources, including Google Fonts. When your browser requests resources from a third-party service, that service may receive technical information associated with the request.

Cerasus does not control the privacy practices of external services and recommends reviewing their respective privacy policies if you have concerns about how those services process information.

7. External Links

The website may contain links to external websites, services or platforms. Cerasus is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of websites that are outside of the Cerasus website.

8. Children's Privacy

The website is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

Because the current website does not require registration or submission of personal information, there is no dedicated collection of children's personal data through the site's current functionality.

9. Data Security

Reasonable care is taken to keep the website and its associated systems secure. However, no website or method of transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the Cerasus website changes or if additional services are introduced.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of the Cerasus website, you may contact us at applepark.contact@icloud.com .
